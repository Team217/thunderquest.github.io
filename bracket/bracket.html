Set up the bracket

One team per line: number, then an optional name. Teams are paired in the order you list them (1st vs 2nd, 3rd vs 4th, and so on).

Pull from the scores page: choose the built _site/index.html (or data.json), then how many top teams you want.

If you list fewer teams than the bracket size, the empty spots become byes (the team just moves on).
